How near limit is checked

  1. Each parameter has a near-limit zone: the last 20% of the way from nominal to each limit. For squeegee speed (nominal 70, spec 56–84 mm/s), near LSL is 56.0–58.8 mm/s.
  2. Every unit rule is also checked as a near-limit variant, with the same three checks: 2× or more, Fisher p < 0.01, 20% of boards with the defect.
  3. Boards under a linked near-limit rule met spec, so they go to Review, not Hold. Boards whose supporting measurement is out of spec are held.
