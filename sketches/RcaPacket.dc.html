How a root cause is linked

  1. The Failure Library gives the path: rule → mechanism → defect. Rules with OR are split into unit rules joined only by AND.
  2. The RCA model scores every board and works out which parameters raised its risk. For each unit rule:
    • Boards with the defect the model attributes to it: 20% or more
    • Predicted mechanism matches the path: 50% or more of those boards
    • Model agrees with inspection on the batch: 90% or more
Linked: passes all three. Contributing: under 20% on its own, but the mechanism's root causes together reach 20%.
What-if re-scores the boards with the root cause at nominal. Model risk bands: High 5% or more, Medium 1–5%, Low under 1%.
